Abuja, Nigeria

Abuja is Nigeria's capital city, after Lagos. Nicknamed the "Centre of Unity", it is not iself a state in the same way that otheres are, so Nigeria is said to have 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja. It is found at the heart of the country, symbolizing it's belonging to and being reachable by, every Nigerian. This is the place where the famous Zuma Rock is located, and, of course, the Aso Rock--Nigeria's own White House. It houses almost all the major federal government headquaters, from the Central Bank to the Road Safety COmmission. Abuja is a very beautiful city, with a lot to see, and a lot to do; the land the people, the food, the night life, the landmarks, all of it is a sight to see.
